**DRAFT — pending legal review.** This is a short, generic placeholder written for internal/product use. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and will be replaced with final, counsel-approved language before this product is used by real customers in production.

**What we collect.** Account information you give us (such as your name, email, and business details), data from the Meta ad account you connect (campaign, spend, and performance data reported by Meta), and basic usage analytics about how you use the app.

**How we use it.** To provide and operate the service, to generate the AI-powered summaries and recommendations you see in the app, to communicate with you about your account, and to maintain and improve the product.

**Sharing.** We do not sell your data to third parties. We share data only with service providers (such as our AI and infrastructure providers) as needed to operate the service, and with Meta to the extent required to read and act on the ad account you connect.

**Your choices.** You can disconnect your Meta ad account and revoke A4M's access at any time, update your account information, and request deletion of your account, as described in the Settings tab.

This placeholder does not yet address every topic a final privacy policy should cover (for example, detailed retention periods, international data transfers, or specific regulatory disclosures). Those will be added once this document is reviewed by an attorney.
